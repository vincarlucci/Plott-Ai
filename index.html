03 // GENERATED CELL MODULES

Modules no human has wired

Our generative models produce the building blocks of production itself: pick, fit, fasten, join, test and handle modules — each one generated complete for a single process, each one different. Not machines from a catalog, but modules that were grown around your product.

  • Control is generated. PLC and HMI code, fieldbus configuration, safety logic — every program proven against the real cell in simulation before a controller is powered on.
  • Physical interaction is learned. How a gripper touches, presses and joins — the AI rehearses contact, force and material behavior in simulation, then encodes it into deterministic control.
  • Electrics are generated. Wiring harnesses, terminal lists, I/O mapping and cable routing are designed by the model — every wire sized, mapped and traceable.
  • Documentation ships with the module. Schematics, BOM, commissioning and maintenance manuals are emitted by the same system. Nothing exists that is not documented.
